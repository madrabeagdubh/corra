An Fhaiche

Walk within three tiles of your partner and swords are drawn; get more than five away and it's over.

Field: tap a tile to walk there, or drag a route with your finger (tap off it to stop). A dim line shows the walk to where your route begins, and in a fight it must begin within two tiles of you. The Line button switches to walking the shape relative to where you stand. Tap your partner to strike; from two tiles off it's a lunge, for two breath.

Brooch: ring hops a tile, moon strikes. Strike while you walk and the cut rides on the stride; the walk carries on. A quick second strike comes back the other way; a patient one is the full cut.

Breath (the gold pips): a swing costs one, and you have as many as you have hearts. A hit that catches them mid-move gives one back. Run dry and you're winded for a moment. On your last heart the moon glows red: one blow they can't parry, worth two hearts.

Steel: the Blades button swaps wood for steel. You have six hearts, as do they, blows roll dice (yours 1d8 plus timing: a patient cut +2, a mid-wind-up catch +2, a backhand or a cut on the move −1), your breath is capped by your hearts, and as you lose hearts you slow down. There is blood. A blow of three hearts or more opens a wound that drips a heart every 12 seconds: stand still for 2.5 seconds to press it shut, or use a bandage (helpless for a moment). Bleeding slows your breath. The teacher stays wooden.

Teacher: she parries everything and never hurts you. Land the strikes she asks for and she shows you the next thing. Swordsman is a real partner. The others want different tactics:

Axeman: slow, hits three tiles at once for two hearts. Don't stand beside him; get round him. Spearman: strikes two and three tiles out in a line and the thrust knocks you back two tiles; get too close and he bashes you with the shaft, back into his reach. He backs away, and throws from range. Shieldman: blocks from the front; his blue-white bash stuns you, then he rushes with everything. Cut him from the side or behind, and don't get bashed. The swordsman and axeman read your route: if it takes you beside them, the red tile appears ahead of you (tap off the route to get out). All aim where you are going; the swordsman follows a miss at once, and the spearman throws from range.
